About

Kala means black. We started with one cup of it.

A simple idea

Kala Café began with a plain belief: a good cup of coffee needs good beans, careful hands and a little time. No syrups to hide behind, no rush.

Today we serve the same two kinds of coffee we started with. Black, for people who want the bean. White, for people who want it softened with milk.

How we got here

  • 2019We open with a single espresso machine and eight seats.
  • 2022We start roasting our own beans in small batches.
  • 2025A bigger room, a full bakery and a weekly book club.

What we care about

Honest sourcing

We buy directly from small farms and pay fair prices.

Less waste

Coffee grounds go to local gardens. Cups are reusable or compostable.

Good people

We train our baristas and pay them well. It shows in the cup.
