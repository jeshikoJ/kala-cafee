Questions

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes, it is free. Ask at the counter for the password.

Can I bring my laptop?

Of course. We have sockets at most tables and welcome long working sessions.

Do you serve vegan options?

Yes. Any white coffee can be made with oat milk, and several bakes are vegan on request.

Do you host events?

We host book clubs, small workshops and private gatherings. Email us for dates.
